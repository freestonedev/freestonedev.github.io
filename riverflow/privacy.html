RiverFlow Privacy Policy

RiverFlow: Stream Flows & CFS, a Garmin watch app by Mosca Made. Last updated November 2026.

RiverFlow has no account and collects no personal data.

What the app uses

The app uses the Connect IQ Communications permission only to fetch public stream gauge readings through your phone's Garmin Connect app, from:

  • U.S. Geological Survey (api.waterdata.usgs.gov)
  • Colorado Division of Water Resources (dwr.state.co.us)

These requests contain only which gauges and dates to look up. They don't include your location, your name or anything else about you.

What stays on your watch

Your chosen rivers, your settings and the last readings are stored only on your watch. Nothing is sent to Mosca Made or anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • No location access
  • No selling or sharing of data

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
